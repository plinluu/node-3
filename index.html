What folks bring us

Three jobs make up 90% of what leaves this shop.

No. 1

"They don't make this part anymore"

Appliance knobs, car clips, vacuum bits, shutter dogs. Bring the broken one in a baggie — we measure it and print you a fresh one. Most from $9.

No. 2

"Can you print a few of these?"

Coaster sets, game pieces, shop jigs, church-fundraiser trinkets. Ten to five hundred at a time, same color, checked by hand. Ask about the 10% quantity break.

No. 3

"I sketched an invention"

Napkin drawings welcome. We print rough versions cheap so you can hold the idea, then refine it. First-look prints from $12.

The fleet: 3 Prusas that never sleep · 1 big-format for helmets & housings · 1 resin machine for tiny detailed work · 1 scanner for copying odd shapes. Filament from US suppliers, dried and stored proper.
